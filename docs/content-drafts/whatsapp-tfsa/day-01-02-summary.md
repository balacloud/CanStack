WHATSAPP DRAFT (review before posting):

Recap for anyone catching up 👋 A TFSA isn't a fancy savings account — it's a wrapper you put around real investments (stocks, ETFs, GICs) so everything inside grows 100% tax-free, forever. Every Canadian 18+ gets new room each January 1st regardless of income — $7,000 for 2026 — and if you've been eligible since 2009 and never contributed, you're sitting on $109,000 of available room. Newcomers: you start earning room from your first day actually living here once you've got real ties (a home, etc.) — no fixed waiting period required.

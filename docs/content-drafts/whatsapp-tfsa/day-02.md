WHATSAPP DRAFT (review before posting):

Yesterday: a TFSA is an investing wrapper, not a savings account. Today's the number that actually matters — how much room do you have? Every Canadian 18+ gets new room every January 1st regardless of income, and for 2026 that's $7,000. If you turned 18 in 2009 or earlier and have never contributed, your total available room right now is $109,000 — yes, six figures, just from unused room stacking up every year. One catch for newcomers: you only start earning room once you're actually a resident of Canada with a valid SIN, so someone who's 35 but only moved here in 2024 doesn't get the full $109K, just what's built up since they landed.

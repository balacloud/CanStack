WHATSAPP DRAFT (review before posting):

TFSA basics from this week, all in one post.

1. What it is: not a bank savings account — it's a wrapper you put around real investments (stocks, ETFs, GICs) so everything inside grows 100% tax-free. You're contributing money you've already paid tax on though — the tax-free part is just the growth, not a deduction like RRSP gives you.

2. How much room you have: every Canadian 18+ gets new room each Jan 1, no matter your income — $7,000 for 2026. Eligible since 2009 and never contributed? You've got $109,000 available. Newcomers: room starts the day you actually become a resident here, not after some fixed wait.
Full breakdown: https://claude.ai/artifact/THfFGixZrQ9uW91dsfdQSH

3. The withdrawal trap: pulling money out doesn't free up room right away, only on Jan 1 of the next year. Put it back before then and you're over-contributing — 1% per month penalty on the excess, plus you have to file a return with CRA. Just needed the cash? Wait for Jan 1. Switching banks? Ask for a direct transfer instead, that never touches your room.
Full breakdown: https://claude.ai/artifact/WXPNZugYhtNiizXowNeo7y

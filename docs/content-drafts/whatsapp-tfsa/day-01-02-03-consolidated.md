WHATSAPP DRAFT (review before posting):

🇨🇦 TFSA basics — everything from this week in one post

1️⃣ What it actually is: not a fancy savings account — it's a wrapper you put around real investments (stocks, ETFs, GICs) so everything inside grows 100% tax-free, forever. (Yes, you're contributing money you've already paid tax on 😅 the tax-free part is just the growth, unlike RRSP.)

2️⃣ How much room you have: every Canadian 18+ gets new room each Jan 1, no matter your income — $7,000 for 2026. Eligible since 2009 and never contributed? You've got $109,000 available. Newcomers: your room starts the day you actually become a resident here, not after some fixed wait.
👉 See the real numbers for your situation: https://claude.ai/artifact/THfFGixZrQ9uW91dsfdQSH

3️⃣ The withdrawal trap: pulling money out does NOT free up room right away — only on Jan 1 of the next year. Try to put it back before then and you're over-contributing: 1%/month penalty on the excess, plus you have to file a return with CRA. Just needed the cash? Wait for Jan 1. Actually switching banks? Ask for a direct transfer instead — that never touches your room.
👉 See exactly how this plays out: https://claude.ai/artifact/WXPNZugYhtNiizXowNeo7y

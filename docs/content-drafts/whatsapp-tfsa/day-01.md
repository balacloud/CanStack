WHATSAPP DRAFT (review before posting):

Quick one for today 👋 A lot of us hear "Tax-Free Savings Account" and picture a fancy bank account. It's not that — it's more like a wrapper you put around your actual investments (stocks, ETFs, GICs, whatever) so that everything growing inside stays 100% tax-free, forever. If you're just parking cash in a "TFSA savings account" earning 2%, you're leaving most of the benefit on the table — the real power shows up when you invest inside it, not just save.

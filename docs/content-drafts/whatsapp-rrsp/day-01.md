WHATSAPP DRAFT (review before posting):

New series, guys 👋 RRSP time. Quick reset since we just did TFSA: a TFSA is tax-free growth, period. An RRSP is different — it's tax-*deferred*. Put money in, and CRA actually lowers your taxable income for that year (it's a real deduction, not a discount trick). But when you take the money out later, it's taxed as regular income then. So the whole game is a bet: you're betting your tax rate in retirement will be lower than your tax rate right now. If your job has RRSP matching, that's free money on top — always worth taking before anything else.

Tomorrow: how much RRSP room you actually have (it's not age-based like TFSA — it's tied to your income, and it carries forward forever if you don't use it).

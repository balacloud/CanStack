# Brand Logo Notes

Last updated: March 20, 2026

## Purpose

This file stores logo-direction decisions, refinement notes, and reusable prompts for CanStack branding work.

Brand exploration is secondary to current product validation priorities, but the guidance is kept here for future retrieval.

## Current Direction

> **Claim:** "The strongest logo direction so far is a simplified stack-based mark with a clean wordmark."
> **Reasoning:** The current explored concepts are strongest when they emphasize:
> - the `stack` idea
> - structure and organization
> - a premium SaaS feel
> They become weaker when they combine too many symbolic ideas at once, such as roof + arrow + maple leaf + stack.
> **Verdict:** [PLAUSIBLE — REASON: This is the strongest direction among the concepts reviewed so far, but final effectiveness still depends on scaling, distinctiveness, and real usage tests.]

## Logo Brief

### Brand role

- CanStack is a SaaS platform for self-employed Canadians managing planning, taxes, deadlines, and compliance.
- The logo should feel credible, calm, structured, and modern.

### Brand personality

- professional
- dependable
- clear
- practical
- Canadian, but not cliché
- operator-focused, not consumer-fintech flashy

### What the logo should communicate

- order
- structure
- confidence
- guidance
- a sense of “stack” or organized layers
- subtle Canadian relevance

### What it should not communicate

- insurance company
- bank
- government agency
- generic freelancer marketplace
- cartoon startup energy
- real estate / home services branding

### Usage requirements

The logo system must work as:

- navbar wordmark
- favicon
- app icon
- social avatar
- monochrome mark
- light and dark background version

### Design constraints

- must be legible at small sizes
- should not rely on long text
- should not depend on gradients to be recognizable
- should work in one color
- should have a standalone symbol plus wordmark

## Preferred Direction

### Keep

- stack/layer motif
- clean `CanStack` wordmark
- navy + teal direction
- subtle structure / confidence cue

### Reduce

- symbolic overload
- literal maple-leaf dominance
- literal house/roof cues
- clutter in small-size versions

## Refinement Prompt

```text
Refine this CanStack logo concept into a cleaner, more premium SaaS identity.

Goal:
Create a professional logo for “CanStack,” a platform for self-employed Canadians managing planning, taxes, deadlines, and compliance.

What to keep:
- the core “stack” idea
- clean modern wordmark: CanStack
- navy + teal brand direction
- subtle sense of structure, confidence, and operational clarity

What to improve:
- simplify the icon
- reduce visual clutter
- do not combine too many symbols at once
- make it feel more like a premium vertical SaaS brand and less like a generic service business

Critical refinement instructions:
- keep the stack/layer motif as the main idea
- use at most 2 symbolic ideas in the icon, not 4
- if using a Canadian cue, make it subtle, not dominant
- avoid a literal house shape if it makes the brand feel like real estate or home services
- avoid overusing arrow + roof + maple leaf + stack all together
- the icon must work at very small sizes (favicon/app icon)
- create a simpler silhouette with stronger recognizability
- prioritize scalability, clarity, and distinctiveness

Design direction:
- modern
- structured
- calm
- trustworthy
- premium
- minimal
- suitable for fintech/compliance-adjacent SaaS

Avoid:
- mascot or illustration style
- long slogan embedded into the main logo
- complex gradients that are required for recognition
- real estate or construction vibes
- government-agency stiffness
- insurance-company clichés

Deliver:
1. Primary logo: icon + wordmark
2. Secondary logo: icon only
3. Monochrome version
4. Small-size version for favicon/app icon
5. Optional tagline lockup kept separate from the main logo

Optional tagline to test separately:
“Planning, taxes, and compliance for self-employed Canadians”

Best icon direction:
- stacked geometric layers
- optional subtle upward/progress cue
- optional subtle Canadian cue through negative space or angle
- overall simpler than current version
```
